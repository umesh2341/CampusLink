CampusLink

Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 12, 2026

CampusLink is an independent student project for campus wayfinding and event discovery. It is not an official application of ITER or SOA University.

Information We Collect

  • Google account information: when you sign in, Google and our authentication provider may provide your name, email address, profile image, and account identifier.
  • App profile information: information needed for your CampusLink profile and optional role requests.
  • Location information: only when you enable live location. Location is used to place you on the campus map and support navigation.
  • Notification information: if you enable alerts, the browser push subscription and notification preferences are stored so alerts can be delivered.
  • Content you submit: events, notices, feedback, and related information you choose to provide.

How We Use Information

We use information to authenticate users, provide maps and navigation, show campus events and notices, manage optional notifications, respond to feedback, and maintain the service.

Sharing and Service Providers

CampusLink uses third-party services including Google for authentication, Supabase for authentication and data services, and hosting/backend providers needed to operate the application. We do not sell personal information.

Location and Notifications

Both live location and browser notifications are optional. You can disable location access in your browser settings and disable notifications through your browser or device settings. Location sharing stops when you turn off the live tracker.

Data Retention and Deletion

We retain information only as long as reasonably necessary to operate the service, provide requested features, resolve issues, and meet security or legal obligations. To request deletion of your account data, contact the project owner through the project repository or the contact method provided with your deployment.

Security

We use reasonable safeguards, but no online service can guarantee absolute security. Do not submit sensitive information that is not needed for CampusLink features.

Changes

This policy may be updated as the project changes. The updated version will be published on this page with a new date.

CampusLink is an independent student project. This page is provided for application transparency and is not legal advice.
